Skills Being Evaluated

7 Core Competencies

Your responses are evaluated across foundational conversational Hindi annotation capabilities according to Svara linguistic standards.

hearingListening Comprehension

Accurately deciphering natural speech across different acoustic clarity levels and speaker speaking rates.

spellcheckWord-Level Accuracy

Verbatim capture of spoken Hindi inflections, Devanagari orthography (matras, anusvara, conjuncts), and colloquial contractions.

publicDialect & Regional Nuances

Recognizing regional accents and colloquial variations (such as Khari Boli and Awadhi inflections) without over-correcting to textbook Hindi.

translateHindi-English Code-Switching

Transcribing modern conversational loanwords in Latin script vs. phonetically transliterated Devanagari per guidelines.

edit_notePunctuation & Formatting

Correct use of Purna Viram (।), commas, question cadences, and sentence boundary segmentation.

noise_awareHandling Unclear Audio

Proper tagging of canonical non-speech events ([inaudible], [crosstalk], [cough], [background_noise]) without guessing inaudible phonemes.

checklistAnnotation Guideline Adherence

Strict compliance with task instructions, boundary timestamps, and formatting standards throughout all audio segments.

Assessment Environment & Rules

av_timer
Duration & Timer

35-minute time limit. The timer starts only when you enter the actual test player, not on this briefing screen.

replay
Audio Replays & Speed

Up to 3 replays per audio clip with variable playback speeds (0.75x, 1.0x, 1.25x) to catch rapid speech segments.

swap_horiz
Free Clip Navigation

Freely navigate between all 8 clips, edit transcriptions, and flag difficult items for review before submitting.

sync
Autosave & Draft Recovery

Drafts persist automatically every 10 seconds. Network hiccups or reload will safely preserve your local inputs.
