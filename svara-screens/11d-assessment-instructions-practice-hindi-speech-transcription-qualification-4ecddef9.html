Core Indic Transcription Protocol

Standard Indic Spec v3.2
Rule A • Verbatim spellcheck

Verbatim Transcription

Preserve spoken words exactly as articulated. Do not paraphrase, grammaticalize, or drop colloquial inflections.

lightbulb Spoken "पे" is kept as "पे", not normalized to "पर".
Rule B • Punctuation edit_note

Punctuation & Purna Viram

Conclude declarative Hindi sentences with Purna Viram (।). Standard commas (,) for natural breath pauses; question/exclamation marks only when clearly intoned.

rule End sentences with । instead of Western full stops (.).
Rule C • Speech Hesitations record_voice_over

Fillers, Stutters & False Starts

Capture organic speech hesitations using standard tokens: [um], [uh], or Indic [हम्म]. Preserve repeated false starts.

repeat Repeated speech like "हम हम जाएंगे" must be transcribed duplicated.
Rule D • Unclear Audio volume_off

Unclear & Unintelligible Speech

Use approved canonical tags: [inaudible], [crosstalk], [cough], [background_noise]. Never guess words.

warning Do not hallucinate inaudible words; guessing penalizes accuracy.
Rule E • Hinglish translate

Code-Switching & Hinglish

Standard technical loanwords spoken in English retain Latin script ("login", "app", "OTP", "verify"). Colloquial Hindi expressions retain Devanagari.

check_circle Write Latin "account"; never Devanagari "एकाउंट".
Rule F • Dialect & Honorifics mic_double

Honorifics & Regional Dialect

Retain honorifics like "जी" (separated by a space) and valid regional vocabulary (Awadhi/Bhojpuri verbal suffixes) without forcing standardized textbook Hindi.

star Write "रमेश जी" with space; preserve verbal marker differences.

1 Colloquialism & Regional Slang

graphic_eq Spoken: "अरे भाई कल तो वो स्टेशन पे मिला ही नहीं..."
check_circle CORRECT TRANSCRIPTION
"अरे भाई कल तो वो स्टेशन पे मिला ही नहीं"

Preserves exact colloquial tokens ('वो', 'पे') without grammar cleanup.

cancel INCORRECT (FAIL)
"अरे भाई, कल तो वह स्टेशन पर मिला ही नहीं।"

Error: Normalizing 'वो' to formal 'वह' and 'पे' to 'पर' fails verbatim guidelines.

2 Bilingual Hinglish Code-Switching
headphones Audio Snippet #EX-02
graphic_eq Spoken: "मेरा account verify नहीं हो रहा please check करो"
check_circle CORRECT TRANSCRIPTION
"मेरा account verify नहीं हो रहा please check करो"

English loanwords kept in Latin Roman script, Hindi in Devanagari.

cancel INCORRECT (FAIL)
"मेरा एकाउंट वेरीफाई नहीं हो रहा प्लीज चेक करो"

Error: Devanagari transliteration of technical nouns ('एकाउंट') violates Spec v3.2.

3 Coughs & Inaudible Acoustic Tags

check_circle CORRECT TRANSCRIPTION
"मुझे [cough] मतलब समझ नहीं आया [inaudible] क्या बोला"

Approved square tags applied without hallucinating inaudible speech.

cancel INCORRECT (FAIL)
"मुझे मतलब समझ नहीं आया उन्होंने क्या बोला"

Error: Guessing words ("उन्होंने") and omitting [cough] fails acoustic guidelines.
