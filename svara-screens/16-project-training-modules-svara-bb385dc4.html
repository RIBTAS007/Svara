Lesson 2.2: Dialectal Vowel Lengthening & Retroflex Flaps

The colloquial Eastern Hindi-Bhojpuri continuum retains non-standard vocalic duration and distinctive verb inflections. Contributors must capture natural phonological variations rather than forcing standard Hindi grammatical concordance.

gavel

Core Orthographic Directives

verified Rule 1: Terminal Flap & Lengthening

Transcribe dialectal terminal auxiliary markers verbatim as uttered (e.g., -बावे rather than standard -है).

verified Rule 2: No Pronoun Sanitization

Never convert dialectal plural pronouns (e.g., हमनी के / हमन के) into Khariboli Hindi हम लोग.
