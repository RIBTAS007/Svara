About this project

Project ID: PRJ-8842

Purpose: This initiative aims to train state-of-the-art conversational Indic AI speech models to accurately recognize and comprehend everyday colloquial Hindi and eastern regional dialects as they are actually spoken in real life.

What you'll do: As an approved contributor, you will listen to authentic real-world telephony audio snippets, transcribe conversational turns into verbatim Devanagari, faithfully preserve regional vocabulary and idioms (Bhojpuri, Awadhi, Maithili), and tag speaker overlaps and acoustic background events.

Why dialect matters: Standard speech-to-text models routinely fail on non-urban Indian speakers because regional vernacular possesses unique verbal inflections, polite plural agreement, and uncodified phonetic shifts. Your expert contributions directly bridge this digital divide.

Domain & Medium Conversational Telephony Dual-speaker phone conversations
Target Dialect Group Bhojpuri & Eastern Hindi Purvanchal & Western Bihar cluster
Primary Focus Verbatim Phonetic Fidelity Zero normalization policy

Project Guidelines & Standards

v2.4

Published Nov 12, 2026 • Curated by Indic Computational Linguistics Team

View Full Changelog open_in_new
1 Strict Verbatim Devanagari Transcription
Core Directive

Never autocorrect colloquial filler or phonetic stutters into standard Khariboli. Transcribe phonetic stutters, filler tokens (हँ..., मतलब), and non-standard vowel lengthening exactly as uttered.

2 Regional Retroflex & Inflection Rules
Phonological Standard

Follow Rohtas-Bhojpuri virama (्) and nukta (़) spelling conventions consistently for phonemic retroflex flap representation (ड़/ढ़) and regional loanwords without substituting modern Hindi approximations.

3 Timestamp Boundary Tightness
Acoustic Alignment

Speech boundary segments must be pinned within ±20ms of acoustic onset and offset. Do not leave audible breath bursts, consonant clicks, or vocalized plosives unbracketed.

Bhojpuri Orthographic & Pronoun Conventions

Refer to the phonetic dictionary lookup table below for homophonic variants in Eastern Uttar Pradesh and Western Bihar audio recordings:

राउर(Polite 2nd)
तोहार(Familiar)
बाटे(Is/Exists)
हवे(Dialectal)
इहाँ(Proximal)
उहाँ(Distal)
केकरा(Whom)
जेकरा(Whomever)

Standard Acoustic Annotation Tags

[applause]Audible collective clapping or cheering
[laughter]Vocalized chuckles, giggles, or amusement during conversational turn
[telephony_static]Carrier line packet drop, high background hum, or severe line jitter
[speaker_overlap]Simultaneous speech by secondary speaker exceeding 250ms

Quality & Adjudication Thresholds

Tasks undergo automated Levenshtein-WER distance validation against consensus baselines. Annotators whose rolling consensus score drops below 92.0% are temporarily routed back to refresher modules.

Target Consensus: ≥ 94.0%
WER Ceiling: < 4.5%
