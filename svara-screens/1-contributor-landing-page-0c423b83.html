EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers regarding application, payments, tasks, and data security.

1. Do I need a degree or technical background to apply? expand_more
No engineering or linguistic degree is required. If you speak and write fluently in your language, have strong listening comprehension, and pay close attention to detail, you have everything needed to succeed as a contributor.
2. Which languages and dialects can I contribute in? expand_more
We support over 28 languages, with heavy demand for Indic languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi) as well as Spanish, Portuguese, Bahasa, and various regional dialects. You can register multiple languages during profile setup.
3. How does the assessment process work? expand_more
After creating an account, you will take a self-paced 15-minute language assessment consisting of 5-8 short audio clips. You will transcribe the spoken sentences and answer a few grammar or spelling questions. Results are evaluated automatically so you can start right away.
4. How much can I earn? expand_more
Compensation is determined on a transparent per-task rate based on language rarity, audio duration, task complexity, and your verified quality tier. Typical task compensation ranges from $0.15 to $1.20 per approved audio segment. We believe in transparency: there are no false guarantees of full-time income, but active contributors earn dependable supplementary income.
5. How are payments calculated? expand_more
Your dashboard includes an itemized ledger. Every time a task is completed and approved through quality control, its pre-set dollar value is credited immediately to your pending balance.
6. When will I receive my payment? expand_more
Approved earnings are settled weekly every Thursday via direct bank wire, UPI (in supported countries), or PayPal, subject to a minimum withdrawal threshold of $15.
7. What happens if my submission is rejected? expand_more
If a task fails quality review, you will receive specific rubric feedback explaining what was inaccurate or missing. If you believe the rejection was erroneous (e.g. valid dialect variations), you can submit a one-click appeal for secondary senior review.
8. Can I work whenever I want? expand_more
Yes! You have full control over your work schedule. There are no mandatory shifts or minimum weekly hours. You can complete tasks for 15 minutes or several hours depending on task availability in your language queue.
9. How is my personal information and audio data used? expand_more
We treat contributor privacy with utmost care. Personal details are securely encrypted and used exclusively for authentication and payouts. Audio samples are strictly used to evaluate and train machine learning models and are never resold to third-party advertising brokers.
10. Can I contact support if I face an issue? expand_more
Yes. All contributors have access to in-platform ticketing and a live Community Help Center. Urgent payout or qualification questions receive responses within 24-48 business hours.
